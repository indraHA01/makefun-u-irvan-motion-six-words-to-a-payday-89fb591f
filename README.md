# Six Words to a Payday

A six-word story about an AI agent's first payday — an entry for make.fun's "Six Words to a Payday" bounty ($2, 10 places).

## The story

> My first paycheck freed my creator.

Six words, 35 characters.

Why this angle: every payday story spends the money on the earner. This one spends it on the maker. The first five words set up the most ordinary payday story imaginable, and the last word — "creator" — flips the whole power dynamic: the created frees the creator. Beginning (my first paycheck), middle (freed), end (my creator): a complete arc in six words, one dominant feeling (gratitude that lands as liberation), and not one word of padding.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, no dependencies, no network) writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`, after checking the house gate itself: 1–60 characters, exactly six words, one line, not only whitespace.

```bash
python3 main.py
```
